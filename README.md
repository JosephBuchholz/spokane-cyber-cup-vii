# spokane-cyber-cup-vii
An extracurricular assignment for CPT_S 427: participation in the Spokane Cyber Cup VII

## Reflection

The Spokane Cyber Cup is an annual cybersecurity Capture the Flag (CTF) event. The goal is to solve as many cybersecurity related problems in a limitted amount of time. There were a wide range of challenges including ones related to web technology, cryptography, physical lock picking, OSINT, and reverse engineering. In total there were about 75 challenges to solve in around 6-8 hours. The goal was not only to try to win, but also to learn along the way.

For this event, I tried to leverge my technical abilities while also challenging myself with new areas. In particular, since I am a computer science major, I focused on some of the tasks related to web hacking, GitHub actions, and coding. The web challenges included actions such as having to modify the DOM with the inspector and reading frontend JavaScript code to find volunerabilities that could be exploited via the web console. Additionally, I spent a good amount of time trying to crack some of the cryptographic ciphers including a Vigenère cipher.

I was part of a two person team. In terms of contribution, we were about equal with me scoring 7550 (21 challenges) and my teammate scoring 6637. In total we got 14187 points and placed in 22nd out of 47 teams (though there was a bit of a management mistake with the lower and upper divisions and thus we actually likely placed higher than that). Concidering that we were only a two person team and many other teams had 4 or 5 members, I think that we did quite well.
